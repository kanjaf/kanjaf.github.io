Marketing Campaign Analysis

Tools: Excel · SQL · Power BI

This project analyzed multi-channel marketing campaigns to evaluate performance across paid channels. The goal was to identify which campaigns were driving profitable conversions and which were wasting budget due to low efficiency.

Problem Statement

The marketing team was making decisions based on traffic volume rather than profitability, leading to inefficient budget allocation across campaigns.

Key Highlights

  • Calculated CTR, conversion rate, CPA, and ROAS across campaigns.
  • Identified underperforming channels with high spend but low return.
  • Segmented campaigns by channel performance and profitability.
  • Provided recommendations for budget reallocation to high-ROI campaigns.

Outcome

The analysis helped shift focus from vanity metrics (traffic) to financial efficiency, enabling better allocation of marketing spend and improved overall campaign ROI.
